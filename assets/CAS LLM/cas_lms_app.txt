Project Title: CAS - Comprehensive Learning Management System (LMS) & Institute ERP

=========================================
1. Normal User Perspective (App Overview)
=========================================
CAS is a massive, full-scale Learning Management System designed to digitize and manage an entire educational institute. From live online Zoom classes to complete student performance tracking, it brings everything under one roof.

Key Features for Users:
* Multi-Role Portals: Users can securely join the platform as either a "Teacher" to create and sell courses, or as a "Student" to learn [cite: 1_5.jpeg].
* Comprehensive Student Dashboard: Students have a personalized hub showing their major (e.g., "Computer Science Year 3") and quick access to modules like "My Assignments", "IQ Test", "Workshop Tracker", and "Study Groups" [cite: 6_2.jpeg].
* Interactive Learning & Quizzes: A gamified learning area ("Let's Learn Today") tracks the student's "Total Score" and offers specific language modules like "Java Programming", "Dart Language", and "Python Basics" with progress indicators [cite: 3_4.jpeg].
* Assignment Management: Students can easily "Search assignments" and jump into tasks categorized by difficulty levels like "Beginner" [cite: 2_4.jpeg].
* Attendance & Leave Tracking: Users can view a detailed, day-by-day attendance record marking them as "Present" or "Absent" [cite: 5_5.jpeg]. Additionally, they can track "Leave Requests" and monitor their status (e.g., "Pending" or "approved") [cite: 4_4.jpeg].
* Virtual Classes: Fully integrated with Zoom to facilitate live online lectures and interactive sessions.

=========================================
2. Developer Perspective (Technical Details)
=========================================
Project Overview: 
A large-scale, enterprise-grade mobile application acting as a complete ERP for educational institutes. 

Technical Implementation:
* Complex Role-Based Architecture: Engineered multiple distinct interfaces. The "Super Admin Dashboard" controls high-level modules like "Fee History", "Fee Defaulter", and "Student Management" [cite: 8_2.jpeg], while the Admin portal handles "Course Detail" and "Add Instructor" workflows [cite: 7.jpeg].
* Third-Party API Integration: Successfully integrated the Zoom API to handle live video lectures, scheduling, and real-time virtual classroom sessions directly within the app environment.
* State Management & Dynamic UIs: Handled complex, nested state management to ensure real-time updates across the app, such as instantly reflecting a leave request's approval status [cite: 4_4.jpeg] or updating the student's quiz points [cite: 3_4.jpeg].
* Scalable Data Architecture: Designed a robust database schema to link students to their specific study groups, assignments, attendance logs, and fee payment portals seamlessly [cite: 5_5.jpeg, 7.jpeg].
